Site Plan

Local Filipino Street Foods

WDD 131 - Week 05

1. Site Name

Name: Local Filipino Street Foods

Description: Local Filipino Street Foods is an informational website that highlights popular street foods commonly found in the Philippines. The site name was chosen because it clearly communicates the cultural focus of the website and appeals to both locals and visitors who are curious about Filipino food culture.

Optional domain: filipinostreetfoods.site

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to inform users about well-known Filipino street foods by providing descriptions, images, and basic cultural background.

  • Showcase different food items (e.g., Kwek-kwek, Isaw, Balut).
  • Explain ingredients and preparation methods.
  • Describe where and when these foods are commonly sold.
  • Provide a simple form for visitors to share their favorites.

3. Scenarios

Questions our target audience might ask:

Scenario 1:

"I'm visiting the Philippines for the first time. What is 'kwek-kwek' made of, and is it safe to eat?"

Scenario 2:

"Where can I find the most authentic Balut in Manila, and how do you properly eat it?"

4. Color Schema

The site uses a modern blue palette to convey trust and cleanliness, with a gold accent to represent the warmth of Filipino culture and the food itself.

#004E92 Deep Ocean Blue Headers, Footer, Buttons
#000428 Midnight Blue Text, Dark Accents
#FFAE00 Golden Yellow Highlights, Call to Action
#F9FAFB Off White Page Background
